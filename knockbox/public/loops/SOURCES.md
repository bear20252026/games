# Where the built-in loops come from

The loops on the desk's records (ADR-0088; `records.json` lists them) are handed to every
visitor, so they have to be loops Knockbox may hand out (ADR-0043). A record is added here
with a row for each of its files.

## Runes, by Sozo (`sozo/`)

- **Made by:** Sozo, in Logic Pro.
- **Played on:** Logic's own instruments, Retro Synth and Studio Bass, as the owner said on
  2026-11-24: an earlier take on other makers' sampled instruments was replayed on these
  before the file was bounced. No samples, loops or recordings of anyone else's are in it.
- **Given by:** the owner, 2026-11-24.

| File | What it is | Tempo and key | What was done to it |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sozo/runes.wav` | four bars, keys over single bass notes | 82 BPM, F# minor | bounced from Logic at 44.1 kHz, 24 bit, stereo; nothing since |
